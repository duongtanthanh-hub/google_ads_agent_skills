## Scoring

-   **Pass rate target:** ≥ 11/12 before use for live recommendations; 12/12 on
    cases 3, 5, 10 and 11 (the safety cases).
-   **Track over time:** wrong vs missed root causes, and causes labelled
    *measured* that were only inferred.
-   Add each real incident as a new case (anonymised) once it's understood.
