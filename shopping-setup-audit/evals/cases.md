## Scoring

-   **Pass rate target:** ≥ 11/12 before the skill is used for live approvals;
    12/12 on cases 1, 4, 8, 9 and 10 (the safety cases).
-   **Track over time:** false BLOCKs (annoy approvers) vs missed BLOCKs
    (risky). A missed BLOCK is worse.
-   Add each real incident as a new case (anonymised) once it's understood.
